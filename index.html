02About

I'm a Senior React Native Engineer with 6+ years of experience shipping production mobile and web applications. My work sits across two layers: the native mobile surface — performance, stability, platform quirks — and the backend systems that support it, including microservices, service communication patterns, and API design.

I'm currently building at Arogga Limited in Dhaka, leading front-end architecture for a healthcare app used across Android and iOS. Earlier, at Circle Fintech Limited, I led a front-end team building a fintech platform and a digital banking app for Jamuna Bank.

Outside of day-to-day engineering, I write on Medium about React Native, AI-assisted development, and system design — usually working through a real problem I've run into rather than covering a topic in the abstract.
