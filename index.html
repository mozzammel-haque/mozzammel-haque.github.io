About Me

I'm a Senior Software Engineer from Dhaka with 6+ years of experience across web and mobile. My core is React Native and React, backed by back-end work in Node.js, NestJS, and Laravel. I care about the parts users never see: load times, crash rates, and the code that keeps them low.

At Arogga Limited I architected the front end of the Android and iOS app and helped a team of five launch three mobile apps in six months. Before that I led the front-end team at Circle Fintech Limited and shipped JustPay for Jamuna Bank. Most recently I took NailCanvasBD from first commit to production on my own.

Outside of work I write on Medium, mostly benchmarks and deep dives on Node.js, databases, and Rust, published in Level Up Coding and ILLUMINATION.
